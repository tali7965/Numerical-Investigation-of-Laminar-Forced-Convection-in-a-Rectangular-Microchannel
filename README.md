**Author:** Ali Taheri  
**Discipline:** Mechanical Engineering / Heat Transfer  
**Project:** Computational Fluid Dynamics (CFD) & Heat Transfer Analysis  
**Software:** ANSYS Fluent  

---

## Overview

This repository contains the comprehensive 18-page technical and academic report investigating three-dimensional laminar forced convection and hydrodynamic boundary layer development within a rectangular microchannel ($W = 0.2\text{ mm}$, $H = 0.1\text{ mm}$, $L = 10\text{ mm}$, hydraulic diameter $D_h = 133.3\ \mu\text{m}$).

### Key Highlights
- **Mesh Independence Study:** Evaluated 5 progressively refined grids (291,348 to 704,627 elements) assessing residual convergence, pressure drop, wall temperatures, and convective heat transfer coefficients.
- **Parametric CFD Simulations:** Analyzed Reynolds numbers ($Re = 100, 200, 300$), longitudinal/cross-sectional static temperature and velocity contours, and axial centerline temperature profiles.
- **Theoretical Validation:** Validated against classical analytical relations (Kays & London, Incropera) for laminar duct flow under uniform heat flux, examining thermal entrance effects and hydrodynamic entrance length ($L_{fd,h} \approx 0.05 \cdot Re \cdot D_h$).
